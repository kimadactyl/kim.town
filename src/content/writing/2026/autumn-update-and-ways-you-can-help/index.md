We’ve not given an update on what’s going on behind the scenes at Geeks for Social Change for some time. Over the last six months we’ve been building sustainable structures: a proper offer for [PlaceCal](https://placecal.org/), a new team of volunteers who are taking over vital operations, and a new consultancy offer that will walk you through our approach to building technology that puts communities at the centre.

## Becoming a collective again

Our goal this year has been to de-centre Kim as founder and make GFSC a proper collective, and we’re really happy that this is now happening.

First of all, we’d really love to welcome Ben, our blog editor, as a CIC director. Ben has been transformational in giving the blog a voice and a direction and keeping the cadence going, and it’s now starting to get a life of its own. They’ve also been running the [podcast](https://gfsc.community/alternatives-to-an-apocalypse-talking-to-friedrich-kirschner/), and we’re sure you’re now well accustomed to their voice and their articles on here. So welcome, Ben.

Blue, who joined us as an intern [writing about health commissioning](https://gfsc.community/how-health-technology-is-replacing-care-with-surveillance/), is helping develop our consulting offer, which you’ll start to hear more about in November and the new year. This is on top of the existing work from Katja and Peter, hosting our meetups and doing our finances respectively. There are also a few people still behind the scenes who we’ll be excited to announce in the coming weeks. Thank you to everyone who has got involved.

The result of all of this is that we are starting to develop named roles, each with a specific domain. We’ve created an operating model that lets me stay in a leadership role while giving people autonomy over their own area. We’ve been running regular meetings every Monday, with a monthly strategic session, and overall we’re starting to approach something like a sociocracy model.

## PlaceCal: Kirkstall in November, more in the new year

PlaceCal is hitting the point where everything is nearly ready to ship, but we feel permanently a tiny bit away from it. As we noted, we [shipped a new directory in June](https://gfsc.community/placecal-v0-28-update-we-now-have-a-directory/). The work since then has been making sure we can onboard organisations in a nice, streamlined way that doesn’t require any one person to be here. We’re also working on a public sales site with a  simple price you can buy it for, which is ready to go once the content is reviewed and we’ve run it past people asking us about it. In fact, if anyone has sales expertise and can help us with this, we’re all ears. We’ve also just shipped a theming layer so that it’s much easier for people to customise the theme to their own needs on our shared installation.

In November we’re hopefully going to get an instance up in Kirkstall with the fantastic [BEAT](https://beatls5.co.uk/) team, and we’ve got a strong, but not quite yet confirmed, candidate for Birmingham and a couple of other places in the new year. If you are a community group looking to use PlaceCal in your neighbourhood, now is a really good time to get in touch: we’re offering early adopter discounts for a little while.

## Trans Dimension site overhaul

The first site to get an overhaul with our new theming layer is The Trans Dimension, which now has better filters, sort and search, a tidied up interface, and a host of performance improvements. You can check it out now. We’re still looking for a source of funds to expand the network nationally and work with the groups in the network – any ideas?

## Prototyping the Community Technology Canvas

We’ve also been thinking about how we can create a process for organisations and communities to take control of technology, and start to build shared understandings of what they want, in a way that can resist big tech. We’re currently prototyping our approach to this, the Community Technology Canvas: a method we’re going to start using to work with everyone from grassroots groups to large institutions, to help them think through and plan the actual outcomes they’re hoping to achieve with technology, and how to map what they do onto that.

Again, if this sounds like something you’d be interested in, including as someone for us to trial it on, get in touch. We’re hoping this goes on the blog as our first e-resource in the coming weeks.

## Mondays at 4pm, and the other meetups

As a reminder, we’re still running regular community events. If you want to come and chat about volunteering or meet the team, you can just come along to any Monday at 4pm: it’s pretty informal. We also run a more strategic monthly session, the GFSC Collective Meetup, on the first Tuesday of the month, and a PlaceCal community organisers drop-in, the most recent of which was this week. All of these are free, online, and on our [Discord](https://discord.gfsc.studio/).

Due to lack of interest we’re going to drop the peer support drop-in, but we’re thinking about redoing it as a monthly social live stream, so keep your eyes peeled for what we do with that.

All this info is on our [Events page](https://gfsc.community/events/).

## Three roles we’d love to fill

There are still a few roles we’re really looking to recruit for, which are now nice, small, bounded responsibilities. If anyone has a little time:

- **Distribution.** We think our blog is great, but we just don’t have the resources we’d like to go out there and tell people about it, and put the effort into spreading the word.
- **Discord Community Management.** We could really do with a dedicated community manager: someone who’d like to help grow the Discord and the community, perhaps help us organise some socials and events, and turn it into a place where people feel welcomed and invited, in line with our goal of supporting community groups.
- **Trans Dimension Community Management.** We have all the tech and interest in place to take it national but we don’t have the people hours to run the network. Do you have a few hours a week to spare helping us grow the directory nationally?
- **PlaceCal development.** We’re always looking for people interested in helping develop and maintain PlaceCal.

## How you can help

So yeah, we’ve got a lot going on. As always, we’re really looking for support. We’ve had a bit of a brutal run on funding bids, and lost our core funder a couple of years ago without replacement. So here’s a bunch of ways you can help out.

**Donate.** We are currently about &#xa3;100 a month short of being financially sustainable. That would be say, twenty people donating &#xa3;5 a month, or two people donating &#xa3;50 a month. You can [set up a monthly donation](https://gfsc.community/#/portal) or [make a one-off one](https://gfsc.community/#/portal/support). We also welcome institutional sponsors, and [have a tier for it](https://gfsc.community/#/portal). We’re a bit overdue putting institutional logos in our footer but if that’s a draw for your organisation let’s chat!

**Get involved.** Everything above: the Monday check-in, the meetups, and the three roles we’re looking to fill.

**Commission us.** If you’re interested in PlaceCal, or in some consultancy using our community technology approach, you know where we are.

**Share.** We hoped that by being primarily based on a website and an email list we’d escape the algorithms, but we discovered a whole new set of algorithms: search engine optimisation, and we are getting pummelled on it. Simply linking to one of our articles from your web page helps us become more visible. Inviting us to speak helps too.

Thank you for reading and supporting us, and for all the kind words we’ve had about our articles over the last few months.
